Can each location have its own domain?
Yes. Each site can use its own domain, or a page on yours.
Who pays: head office or the location?
Either. Head office can pay for every site, or each location can buy its own.
Can we sell sites to our franchisees?
Yes. Head office can become a seller and offer sites under your own brand.
