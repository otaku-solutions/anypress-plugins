How can we help?

Quick answers to the things people ask most. If yours isn't here, email us and we'll help.

Sign in to your website
support@anypress.io

Your account

How do I sign in?

Go to your account page and sign in with the email address you bought your plan with. If you don't know your password, choose "Lost your password?" on the sign-in page and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I open my site's editor?

Sign in to your account page, open My website and choose Log in to your website. It opens your site's WordPress dashboard already signed in, with no second password.

My site isn't ready yet. Is that normal?

A new site is usually ready a few minutes after you pay. We email you when it is. If it's been more than an hour, email us and we'll look right away.

Your domain

What address does my site start with?

Every site starts on a free address ending in anypress.site, with a secure (https) connection. You can keep it or switch to your own domain whenever you like.

How do I use my own domain?

Sign in to your account page and open My website. Under "Use your own domain" you'll find the records to add where your domain is managed (the company you bought it from, or Cloudflare). Add them, then type your domain in the same place. The secure connection is set up for you, and the old address keeps working for a few minutes while it switches.

Billing

How do I cancel?

Sign in to your account page, open My website and choose Manage or cancel plan. Your site stays online until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first website plan starts with [anypress_number of="trialDays"] days free. You add a card when you start, and the plan's price starts after the free days unless you cancel first from your account. It's one free trial per person.

How do I get a refund?

Your first payment for a new plan is covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee. Email support@anypress.io within 30 days with your account's email address and your site name. A refund ends the plan and takes the site offline, so export anything you want to keep first. The details are in our refund policy.

Can I change my plan or size?

Yes. Email us with your site name and what you'd like, and we'll switch it for you. The new price starts from your next renewal.

Looking after your site

Is my site backed up?

Yes. We save a copy of every site each night and keep daily and weekly copies. If something goes wrong, email us and we'll bring it back.

Who looks after security fixes?

We do. Security fixes for WordPress, its plugins and the server are applied for you, so you can get on with your business.

If you sell websites

I sell websites. Where do I sign in?

Sellers and resellers sign in to the dashboard at app.anypress.io, not here. It has every client and site you sell. New to selling? See how selling works.

Where do my clients sign in?

On your own storefront. They never get an AnyPress login, and they never see AnyPress.
