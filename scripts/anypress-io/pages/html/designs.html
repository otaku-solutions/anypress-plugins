any time

Try a new look without starting over.

  1. 1. Pick a look

    Open your site's design screen and click a kit. Colours, fonts and spacing change across every page at once.

  2. 2. Or ask your AI app

    Connect AnyPress to your AI app and ask to see the designs. Each kit shows as a live preview of your own site, right in the chat, with a button to switch it on.

  3. 3. Change your mind

    Your old look is saved when you switch, so you can go straight back. Your words, photos and pages stay as they are.

the extras

Pieces that do something.

Add them to any page. They match your look, work on phones, and only load on the pages that use them.

  • Popup

    A box over the page after a few seconds, on scroll or when someone is about to leave. Good for a signup or an offer.

  • Carousel

    Slides that scroll sideways with arrows and dots, for reviews, projects, team members or photos.

  • Before and after

    Two photos of the same job with a handle to drag between them: a kitchen, a lawn, a haircut, a roof.

  • Countdown

    Days, hours and minutes left until an offer ends or an event starts.

  • Counter

    A big number that counts up as it comes into view, such as years in business or jobs done.

  • Star rating

    Your rating, how many reviews and where they're from, for example 4.9 out of 5 on Google.

  • Map

    Your address on a map with a pin and a link for directions.

  • Location finder

    All your branches next to a map. Visitors search by town or tap “Near me” to find the closest.

  • Notice bar

    One short line across the page, such as holiday hours or free delivery. Visitors can close it.

  • Call and book bar

    A bar along the bottom of the screen with Call and Book buttons, so the main action is one tap away.

  • Age check

    Asks visitors to confirm their age before they see the site, for shops selling age-restricted goods.

  • And the usual pieces

    Headings, text, photos, buttons, columns, galleries and videos, all set up to follow your look.

questions

Good to know.

Do I have to use a kit?

No. Every site starts with the AnyPress theme's own look. Kits are there when you want one made for your kind of business.

Can I change the colours and fonts myself?

Yes. Pick a kit, then change any colour or font in the design screen. Every page follows, because pages use the site's colours and fonts rather than fixed ones.

Will switching break my pages?

No. A kit changes how things look, not what's on the page. Your text, photos, forms and shop stay where they are, and the old look is saved.

Do the extras cost more?

No. Popups, countdowns, maps and the rest come with every site, on every plan.

What happens when I click Try it live?

A full WordPress site with that kit opens in your browser, using WordPress Playground. Click around, open the editor, change anything. Nothing is installed and nothing is saved, and it can take a minute to open.

Can I use the designs on another WordPress site?

Yes. The AnyPress theme and the AnyPress Blocks plugin are free and open source, for any WordPress site.
