Questions.

Can I try it before the beta?

Yes. Open a community site or any design in your browser. It runs on WordPress Playground: nothing is installed and nothing is saved.

What does it cost?

For your own site, you add a card when you start and the first 7 days are free. Cancel before day 8 and you pay nothing. Sellers and resellers book a short call with us to get set up, billing included. After that, plans run from [anypress_price type="website" size="small" period="monthly"] a month for a website to [anypress_price type="everything" size="small" period="monthly"] for everything, and your first payment has a 30-day money-back guarantee. See pricing.

How many places are there?

We're keeping the beta small: 25 sites in total. That's places for 3 sellers or resellers with up to 5 Small sites each, and 10 website owners with one each. Free demos on anypress.io/try have no limit, and everyone else can start at launch on 1 December.

Do my Fluent Pro licences work?

Yes. Sites come with the free versions of the Fluent plugins. Add your Pro licences like on any WordPress site, and the Pro features turn on.

Which WordPress and PHP do sites run?

WordPress 7.1 on PHP 8.3, each site with its own WordPress and database.

What happens to my site when the beta ends?

Pick a plan at today's prices and your site keeps running as it is, on the same address and with its backups. There's nothing to move or rebuild. If you don't pick one, the site is removed after launch.

What if I want to leave?

Download the whole site any time and take it to any host. In wp-admin, Tools > Download my site gives you one zip with WordPress, every file and the database, and it restores on any WordPress host. Sellers and resellers get the same button on each site's page in the dashboard. No lock-in.

Can it run on my own server?

Yes. We host it, or we set AnyPress up on your server. The whole platform is open source.

I build sites for clients. Can I resell?

Yes. Sellers sell from one shop and pay per site; resellers run several brands on one wholesale bill. See how it works.

Can you move my clients' sites too?

Yes. Any WordPress site can come across, yours or a client's, one at a time or several. Each one becomes its own site in your dashboard, with its own domain and backups.

Something else?

Email support@anypress.io and we'll get back to you.
