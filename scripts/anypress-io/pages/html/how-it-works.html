step by step

From picking a site to opening your doors.

1. Pick what you're building and pay

a minute

Choose a website, bookings, a store, courses or everything, and a size. Small suits most new sites. You add a card, monthly or yearly, and the first [anypress_number of="trialDays"] days are free. After that, your first payment is still covered for 30 days. See prices.

2. We build your site

minutes

Nobody has to press a button. As soon as you've paid, your site is being made, and it arrives finished:

  • Pages that already suit your kind of business
  • The tools you picked switched on, like your shop, bookings or courses
  • Your business name, address, time zone and currency filled in
  • A free address to share straight away, with the padlock already on
  • Email that sends from your site, so receipts and bookings reach people

3. You get a welcome email

when it's ready

It links to your account on anypress.io. From there, one click opens your site's editor. There's no second password to set up.

4. Make it yours

an afternoon

Swap in your words, photos and prices. Change colours and fonts in a few clicks. Already have a domain? Type it in, and with supported domain companies it connects in one click. Email starts coming from it too.

5. Open your doors

whenever you like

Share your link and start taking orders, bookings or sign-ups. Payments go straight to your own account, and we never take a cut.

6. We look after it from then on

every day

A copy of your site is saved every night. Security fixes are applied for you. If something goes wrong, email us and we'll help.

questions

Questions about how it works

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the technical part. If you can write an email, you can edit your pages, and we're an email away if you get stuck.

How long does a new site take?

Minutes, not days. You get an email as soon as it's ready. If a build ever fails, we're told straight away and fix it.

Is my site really separate from everyone else's?

Yes. It has its own space and its own WordPress. Another site getting busy or having a problem doesn't touch yours.

Who can see or change my site?

You, and anyone you add. We only step in to keep it running, or when you ask us for help.

Can I bring the site I already have?

If it's on WordPress, yes. How moving works.

Can I take my site with me?

Yes. It's real WordPress, so a full copy can go with you to any host. No lock-in.
