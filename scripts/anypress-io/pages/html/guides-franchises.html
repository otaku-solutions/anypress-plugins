Rolling out 10 locations: a worked example.

How a brand with ten locations can give each one its own site from one starting point, and what it costs.

The plan

  1. Head office applies as a seller and builds one site the way every location should look.
  2. Each location gets a copy, with its own address, hours, staff and bookings.
  3. Locations update their own details. Head office keeps the look and the offers the same.
