AnyPress vs Shopify

Shopify rents you a store. AnyPress gives you one.

Both get you selling online. With Shopify you add paid apps as you grow and sell by their product rules. With AnyPress the tools come with the site, and the site is yours to take anywhere.

Which one is right for you?

Shopify is the better choice if…

You sell at high volume across many sales channels, and want Shopify's in-person checkout and its app store.

AnyPress is the better choice if…

You want one price with the tools included, sell something Shopify may not allow, or want a store you fully own.
