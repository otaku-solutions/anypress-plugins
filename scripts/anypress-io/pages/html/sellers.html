already have clients?

Bring the sites you already look after.

Move each client's site over at your own pace. Their old site keeps running, untouched, until you switch.

1. Get a token

Choose Move a site in on your dashboard. We set up the new site and give you its token.

2. Add Reprint

Install Reprint, WordPress's own free moving plugin, on the client's current site and paste the token in.

3. We copy it across

The whole site comes straight from the old host: pages, products, orders, theme, plugins and photos.

4. Check it, then go live

Look it over on its new address, copy again to catch anything new, then point the domain at it.

On WooCommerce, LearnDash, BuddyPress or another plugin? Each site can switch those to the Fluent plugins in a few clicks. What can be switched.

questions

Questions sellers ask

Do my clients see AnyPress?

No. They buy from your storefront, get emails from your name and manage their site from an account on your shop.

How many sites do I need to start?

None. Start with one. Once you have [anypress_number of="volumeFirstSites"] live sites, every site saves [anypress_number of="volumeMin"]%, and it goes up from there.

How fast is a client's site ready?

Minutes after they pay. The site is copied from your starting site, with their details filled in, and the welcome email goes out from your name.

Can I bring sites I already look after?

Yes. Choose Move a site in on your dashboard, add WordPress's free Reprint plugin to the old site and paste in the token. The whole site is copied across. How moving works.

Do I have to create each client's site by hand?

No. A paid order on your storefront builds the site by itself. Clients who sign up somewhere else can get a site from a form, a payment or your CRM through n8n, Make or Zapier. Create sites automatically. On HighLevel? Add a website to every sub-account.

What if I change my starting site later?

Save the change as a new version on the Templates page, check it, then choose it for new orders. Clients' existing sites aren't touched.

Who supports my clients?

You do the client-facing side, the way you do today. We support you, and we look after the sites themselves.

What does the storefront cost?

Nothing. Your first storefront is included. You pay for each live site you sell.

I run several brands. Is this for me?

Then a reseller account fits better: several storefronts, one wholesale bill at [anypress_number of="wholesalePercent"]% off, and partner keys for each brand. About resellers.
