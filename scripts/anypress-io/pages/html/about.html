WordPress can do anything. We make it easy.

AnyPress is built by Otaku Solutions. We take the hard parts of WordPress off your plate, so you can get on with your business.

WordPress can feel like a giant flea market.

There are thousands of add-ons, and nobody tells you which ones fit together. Then there's hosting, the padlock, backups and email to sort out before you sell a single thing.

So we picked the parts, fitted them together, and made the whole thing something you order instead of something you build. Pick the kind of site you need, and it arrives ready.
