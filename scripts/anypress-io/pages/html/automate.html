Automation questions

Do I need a developer?

No. If your clients buy on your storefront, sites are already created for you. For anything else, add the AnyPress app in n8n, Make or Zapier and pick the step. Any other tool can do the same with a web request.

Where do I get an API key?

Sign in at app.anypress.io and open API keys. The secret is shown once, so put it straight into your tool's saved logins. Resellers can ask us for a partner key, which works on every storefront in the account.

How do I use a template?

Add the AnyPress app to your tool first. In n8n, download the workflow and paste or import it onto an empty canvas. In Make, download the blueprint, create a scenario, then choose Import blueprint from its ... menu. In Zapier, add AnyPress with the invite link and follow the guide: each Zap is a trigger and one AnyPress step. Then pick your AnyPress login on the AnyPress step and turn it on.

What can a new site be told?

Its name, your reference, the client's email and role, which starting site to copy, its size and the client's own domain. The guide for each app lists them, and the API reference has every field.

Which events can start a flow?

Site ready, site failed, domain connected, suspended, resumed, deleted, restored and backup done. Pick one or several on the AnyPress trigger.

What if the flow runs twice?

Always fill in Your Reference with something unique, like the order or deal number. Running Create again with the same reference gets the site already made, and never builds another.

How does the client get into their new site?

Creating the site makes their WordPress login with their email. Your welcome email links to the site's password page, so they choose their own password. Sites sold on your storefront get a one-click sign-in from their account instead.

Can it put the client's own words and logo in too?

Yes. Every site starts from your starting site, and an AI app connected to AnyPress can then change pages, sections, colours and menus for you. See AnyPress in your AI app.

Do my clients see AnyPress?

No. The flows are yours, the emails come from you, and sites sold from your shop show your brand.

Is there an app for HighLevel (GoHighLevel)?

Yes, in early access. Install AnyPress on your sub-accounts, create a client's site from any HighLevel workflow, then start the welcome steps when it's ready. Email support@anypress.io and we'll set you up. You can also start today with HighLevel's own webhook steps: the HighLevel guide in our docs shows how.
