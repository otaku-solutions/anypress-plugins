Member website program: a one-page guide.

How a chamber or association can offer members their own websites, as a benefit or as a new source of income.

How it works

  1. You apply as a seller and get your own shop, under your organisation's name.
  2. You pick which kinds of sites to offer members, and set your prices.
  3. Members buy from your shop. We build and look after each site.
  4. You pay per site, and the price per site drops at 3, 10 and 25 sites.

Two ways to run it

  • As a member benefit: price sites at cost, and point to it when members renew
  • As income: add a monthly margin on each site to fund your programs

Your first month

  1. Pick three members who need a website and offer them a first-month discount.
  2. Mention it in your newsletter and at your next event.
  3. Ask those first members if you can show their sites to others.
