Only your words and photos, and only if you want to. If you can write an email, you can edit your pages. We do the technical part.

Can customers pay a deposit when they book?

Yes. You can take card payment when someone books, so a no-show doesn't cost you the slot.

Can it send my leads to HighLevel?

Yes. AnyPress works with HighLevel, so the people who book or write in can land in your HighLevel contacts too.
