The fine print.

What counts as a sale?

Any plan or email pack someone buys within 30 days of clicking your link. If they clicked someone else's link after yours, the later link gets the sale.

What's the commission worked out on?

[anypress_number of="affiliatePercent"]% of the amount actually paid, after any discount and before tax. Renewals aren't included.

What if they ask for a refund?

If the customer is refunded, the commission on that sale is cancelled. We hold each commission for 30 days, the length of our money-back promise, before it can be paid.

Can I buy through my own link?

No. Your own purchases don't earn commission.

Is there anything I can't do?

Don't bid on the AnyPress name in search ads, send spam, or promise things we don't offer. We may close accounts that do, and unpaid commission from those sales isn't paid.

When do payouts go out?

At the start of each month, by PayPal, once your approved balance reaches $50. Smaller balances roll over to the next month.

Can these terms change?

We'll give you 30 days' notice by email before we change the rate or terms. Questions? Email support@anypress.io.
