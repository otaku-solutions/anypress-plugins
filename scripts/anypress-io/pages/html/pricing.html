Price questions

Why does a shop cost more than a website?

Shops, bookings and members' areas do more work and send more email, so they need more from the computers they run on. The price follows what the site needs.

Which size do I need?

Start with Small. It suits most new sites. If your site gets busy, move up to Medium or Large and nothing about your site changes except the room it has.

What happens if I send more email than my size includes?

Your everyday emails, like receipts and booking confirmations, keep sending. Newsletters to your list pause until the next month or until you move up a size.

Is there a yearly price?

Yes. Paying yearly costs 10 times the monthly price, so you get 2 months free.

Do you charge a fee on sales?

No. The card company charges its normal fee on payments, and we add nothing on top.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first website plan starts with 7 days free. You add a card when you start, and the plan's price starts on day 8 unless you cancel first. It's one free trial per person.

How do I cancel?

From your account, any time. Cancel in the first 7 days and you pay nothing. Within 30 days of your first payment, you get that payment back.

Can you make changes to my site for me?

Yes. Care gives your website time for changes each month, from [anypress_care item="care" show="monthly"] a month, and one-time help to set it up or move it in. See the care page at anypress.io/care.
