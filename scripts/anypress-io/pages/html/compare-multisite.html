AnyPress vs WordPress multisite

Every site on its own, not one network sharing everything.

Multisite runs many sites from one WordPress install and one database. AnyPress gives every site its own WordPress and its own database, so one site never drags down the rest.

What you needWordPress multisiteAnyPress
DatabaseOne, shared by every siteEach site has its own
Plugins and themesInstalled once for the whole networkEach site has its own
One site gets busy or breaksCan slow down or affect the othersThe others carry on as normal
Plugins that don't support multisiteCan't be used, or need workaroundsEvery site is a normal single site
A client wants to leaveThe site has to be pulled out of the networkIt's a normal WordPress site they can take
Security fixes and nightly copiesYour job, for the whole networkDone for you, site by site
being fair

Which one is right for you?

WordPress multisite is the better choice if…

Your sites really do share users and content, like the departments of one organisation, and your team runs the server.

AnyPress is the better choice if…

Each site belongs to a different customer, with its own plugins, its own data and its own bill.
