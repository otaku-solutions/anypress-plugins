AnyPress vs WordPress multisite

Every site on its own, not one network sharing everything.

Multisite runs many sites from one WordPress install and one database. AnyPress gives every site its own WordPress and its own database, so one site never drags down the rest.

being fair

Which one is right for you?

WordPress multisite is the better choice if…

Your sites really do share users and content, like the departments of one organisation, and your team runs the server.

AnyPress is the better choice if…

Each site belongs to a different customer, with its own plugins, its own data and its own bill.
