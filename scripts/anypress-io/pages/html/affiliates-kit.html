Your link.

Sign in to your affiliate dashboard to see your link. It looks like https://shop.anypress.io/?ref=123, where 123 is your number.

You can add ?ref=123 to the end of any page on shop.anypress.io, such as a plan's page, and the sale still counts as yours.

Sharing in several places? Add a name for each one, like &utm_campaign=youtube-oct, so you can tell which one works best. Use lowercase words joined by dashes.
