under the hood

For the technical folks.

The same system, in a bit more detail.

Its own WordPress

Every site runs its own copy of WordPress with its own database. Nothing is shared between sites.

Walled off

Sites can't reach each other or the machine they run on. One site having a bad day stays its own problem.

Sizes we've tested

Small, Medium and Large each get set computing power, memory and storage, sized from load tests.

Copies kept off-site

Nightly copies are stored away from the servers, and we test restoring one every day. Hosting and safety.

An API for everything

Create, change and remove sites with code, or from n8n, Make and Zapier. See the API.

Open source

The software behind AnyPress is open source, so you can read how it works, or run it yourself.

questions

Questions sellers ask

Do my clients see AnyPress?

No. They buy from your storefront, get emails from your name and manage their site from an account on your shop.

What is a starting site?

It's the site every new order is copied from. Use one of ours, or set up your own with your design, pages and plugins, so every client starts with it.

What does the storefront cost?

Nothing. Your first storefront is included. You pay per site you sell.

Can I create sites without a shop?

Yes. Sites can come from a form, a payment or your own app through the API, n8n, Make or Zapier. Create sites automatically.

Who supports my clients?

You do the client-facing side. We support you, and we look after the sites themselves.

Who updates WordPress and the plugins?

We do, for WordPress, our theme and the plugins we include. Every new release is tried on every kind of site before it reaches yours. Plugins you add yourself, you update in a new version of your starting site.

Does every site get the padlock (HTTPS)?

Yes. Every site and every domain your clients connect gets a free certificate, renewed for you.

What happens when a client stops paying?

When their plan is cancelled, the site is backed up and taken offline for you. Nothing to remember.

Can I bring sites I already host?

Yes. Choose Move a site in on your dashboard and the whole site is copied across. How moving works.
