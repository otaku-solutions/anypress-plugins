two minutes

Connect it once.

1. Add AnyPress as a connector

In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. In ChatGPT, turn on developer mode under Settings, Apps and Connectors, then create a connector. In Cursor, VS Code, Devin Desktop, Gemini CLI or any other MCP app, add a remote MCP server. Paste the address below.

2. Sign in and choose what it may do

Your AI app opens our sign-in page. Sign in to AnyPress, tick what the app may see and change, and approve. There's no key or secret to copy.

3. Ask

Type what you need, the way you'd ask a person. The app picks the right steps, and shows your sites right in the chat.

AI app questions

Which AI apps work?

Any app that connects to MCP servers: Claude and ChatGPT (on plans that let you add custom connectors), code editors such as Cursor, VS Code with GitHub Copilot and Devin Desktop, and command-line agents such as Claude Code and Gemini CLI. A tool that can’t open a sign-in page can use your storefront’s API key and secret instead.

Do I need an API key?

No. You sign in to AnyPress and approve, the same way you'd sign in with Google. The app never sees a password or a secret.

Can my clients use it for their own site?

Yes. A website owner adds the /mcp/site address and approves in their site's dashboard. Their app can then change that one site's pages and design, and nothing else.

Does it know my site's designs?

Yes. The app can see every ready-made section and starter page, the 17 design kits made for different kinds of business, the colour and font sets, and the interactive blocks such as carousels, popups, counters and maps. Ask to see them and the app shows each kit as a live preview of your site in that look, right in the chat. It can switch your site to a kit’s look and build pages from that kit’s sections. The old look is saved, so you can go back.

How do I stop it?

Remove the connector in your AI app. Access also ends the moment you leave the team.

Building your own agent?

The connector speaks the open MCP standard, so any agent can use it. The API, SDKs and webhooks are on the developer page.
