questions

Reseller questions

What does it cost?

[anypress_number of="wholesalePercent"]% off our list price for every live site, on one bill each month. A site that goes live mid-month is billed for the days it was live, and suspended sites don't count. You charge your customers whatever you like.

Do my customers see AnyPress?

No. They buy from your storefronts, at your prices, under your brands.

Seller or reseller: which am I?

One business name? You're a seller. More than one? You're a reseller. One application covers both, and we'll set up the right account.

Can each brand sell different sites?

Yes. Each storefront has its own starting site, its own prices and its own domain.

Can I start as a seller and become a reseller later?

Yes. Tell us when you're ready and we'll move your account over.

Can my developers create sites automatically?

Yes, with or without a developer. Partner keys let your code create, change and remove sites for each brand, and no-code tools like n8n, Make and Zapier can do it from a form, a payment or your CRM. See create sites automatically.

Who do I ask?

Email support@anypress.io.
